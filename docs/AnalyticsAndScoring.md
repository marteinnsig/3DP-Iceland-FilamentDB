# Analytics and Scoring

The analytics layer turns raw test data into ranked, explainable decisions.

## Major areas

- Engineering score profiles.
- Rankings dashboard.
- Category rankings.
- Filtered rankings.
- Awards and winners.
- Radar/visual analytics.
- Hidden gem and opportunity detection.
- YouTube opportunity scoring.

## Design goal

Scores should be useful as decision support, not hidden magic. UI panels should explain why a material, video idea, comparison or playlist was recommended.
