# 3DPIceland Platform Architecture

## Materials Platform
Material definitions, metadata, classification and lifecycle.

## Purchasing & Inventory Platform
Suppliers, purchase orders, mixed-category lines, receiving, physical inventory and future cost intelligence.

## Engineering Platform
Tensile, impact, stiffness, calculations, scoring and verification gates.

## Publishing Platform
Website export, reports, certificates and future YouTube production planning.

Each platform grows by extension. Shared identities and verification contracts must remain stable.
