# Verification Center

## Responsibility

Verification Center is the quality gate and Platform Health surface for the full application.

## Current role

It verifies:

- Calculation service availability
- Native material source and MaterialID coverage
- Measurement row alignment
- Tensile, impact, and stiffness calculation migration
- Material Summary Engine
- Website Platform pipeline, charts, radar, renderer, verification suite, and publish readiness

## Rule

No subsystem is considered complete until it has a visible verification gate and the overall report returns PASS.

## Long-term direction

Verification Center should evolve into a Platform Status Dashboard showing Database Platform, Engineering Platform, Website Platform, Reporting Platform, and AI Platform health.
