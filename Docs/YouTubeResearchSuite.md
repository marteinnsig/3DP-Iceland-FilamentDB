# YouTube Research Suite

The YouTube Research Suite is the v25 creator-focused workflow for 3DPIceland Labs.

## Purpose

Convert filament database records into practical YouTube planning outputs:

- Video titles
- Thumbnail ideas
- Comparison opportunities
- Publishing calendar suggestions
- Channel coverage gaps
- Playlist opportunities

## Pipeline

```text
Material data
  → Video candidate generation
  → Advanced title engine
  → Thumbnail research engine
  → Comparison discovery
  → Content calendar planner
  → Channel gap analysis
  → Playlist discovery
```

## Modules

### v25.1 - Real Advanced Title Engine
Generates stronger video title candidates, talking points, hook angles and copy-ready prompts.

### v25.2 - Thumbnail Research Engine
Creates thumbnail main text, layout ideas, reaction/face direction, pattern scoring and top thumbnail recommendation.

### v25.3 - Comparison Discovery
Finds side-by-side video stories such as reinforced vs plain, CF vs GF, ABS vs ASA and similar high-value comparisons.

### v25.4 - Content Calendar Planner
Builds a multi-week content plan while balancing manufacturer diversity and material-family diversity.

### v25.5 - Channel Gap Analysis
Identifies high-potential materials or topics with weak/no channel coverage.

### v25.5.1 - YouTube Research Scrolling Fix
Adds full-page scrolling and local grid scrolling so all YouTube Research sections remain reachable.

### v25.6 - Playlist Discovery
Finds playlist opportunities, playlist completion, missing/next videos and playlist-level strategy.

## Creator rules reflected in the design

- Avoid publishing too many videos from the same manufacturer in a row.
- Avoid long blocks of the same material family.
- Prefer strong comparison stories and data-backed curiosity hooks.
- Treat series discovery as YouTube playlist discovery.
- Keep copy buttons close to practical outputs.
